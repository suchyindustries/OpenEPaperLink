# OpenEPaperLink

This is Skip's personal fork of the OpenEPaperLink project with additional content
and features.  

## Why?

- Provide ability to easily release Betas of new features before merging with main repo.
- Some of my additional content types are of limited interest to the general community.<br>1. NOAA tides: The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA)
is a United States government agency which only provides tide data for US
territories. The vast majority of current OEPL users do not appear to reside
in the US so this content is probably of little interest to them.<br>2.Septic tank monitor (TODO) : Hopefully most people who even have a septic 
tank has one that works better than mine and it doesn't need monitoring!

## Motivation (or a solution in search of a need)

My wife and I vacation near the ocean every year and hike a lot.

It's important to know when high/low tide is since some of the hiking trails and parking lots are flooded at high tide.
